From one quiet idea to one beloved ritual

Slow coffee, made with care.

Lumière began with one idea: a cup of coffee deserves your full attention. We work directly with small farms, roast every week, and brew each cup to order.

Every bean is chosen for character, every roast is tasted by hand, and every cup is finished just for you. It is a simple process, but one we believe should never feel rushed.

Our room is quiet, warm and unhurried. Sit by the window, bring a book, or share a table with someone you like.
